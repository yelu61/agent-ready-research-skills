# Result interpretation prompt

Interpret the supplied result using this structure:

1. direct observation with quantitative support;
2. evidence state: confirmed, supported, exploratory, superseded or blocked;
3. biological interpretation;
4. alternative explanations and design limitations;
5. sensitivity or validation evidence;
6. causal claims that are not justified;
7. exact source files;
8. documents, decisions or author queries that must be updated.
