# Result interpretation prompt

Interpret the supplied result using this structure:

1. direct observation with quantitative support;
2. source provenance, source integrity, execution and technical-validation
   status as separate facts;
3. scope, intended use, analysis mode, requested/authorized claim classes,
   claim authorization, target/achieved validation, lifecycle and readiness;
4. biological interpretation;
5. alternative explanations, falsification tests and design limitations;
6. sensitivity, orthogonal or independent validation evidence;
7. causal, mechanistic, translational or clinical claims not justified;
8. exact artifact, run, spec and readiness IDs plus report hashes;
9. null/conflicting results and uncertainty;
10. documents, decisions or author queries that must be updated.

Do not convert successful execution, statistical significance or a current
readiness declaration into a statement that the interpretation is true.
