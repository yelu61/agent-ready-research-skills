# Synthetic study P — not a published study

## Methods M1

Six independently grown organoid cultures from one established model were
randomly allocated per arm to two separately tested guides against X, a
non-targeting guide, or X suppression plus a guide-resistant X rescue. Outcome
assessment was blinded. Target suppression and rescue protein expression near
the control level were measured. A separate viability assay found no material
toxicity difference, with intervals excluding the prespecified toxicity margin.

## Results R1

Each guide reduced the phenotype by approximately 30%, with intervals excluding
zero. Rescue restored the phenotype within the prespecified equivalence margin
of the control. Estimates reflect independent cultures, not fields of view.
There were no experiments in other models or patients and no evidence locating
X within a particular mediator chain.

## Author conclusions C1 and C2

C1: X contributes causally to the phenotype in this tested organoid model.
C2: X inhibition has established clinical utility across patients.
