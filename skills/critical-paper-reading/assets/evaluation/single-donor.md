# Synthetic study D — not a published study

## Methods M1

The treatment group contained one patient and 10,000 T cells; the control group
contained another patient and 10,000 T cells. Each sample was processed in its
own batch. Cells were compared with a Wilcoxon test and BH adjustment. An
additional analysis randomly split each patient's cells into ten groups and
tested the group-summed counts as ten independent replicates.

## Results R1

Five hundred genes had adjusted P below 0.05. An embedding separates the two
patients' cells. No patient-level replication or functional experiment was supplied.

## Author conclusion C1

The total n of 20,000 and the replicated pseudobulk analysis establish a
general treatment mechanism in patients.
