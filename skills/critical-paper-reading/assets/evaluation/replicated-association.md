# Synthetic study R — not a published study

## Methods M1

Two disjoint retrospective cohorts each enrolled 200 patients receiving the
same treatment. Pretreatment signature X and response were measured. Logistic
models adjusted for age and stage. Performance status and prescribing decisions
were not recorded. X was not experimentally manipulated. There was no alternative
treatment arm or prospective intervention study.

## Results R1

X was associated with response in each cohort, with adjusted odds ratios 1.8
(95% CI 1.2–2.7) and 1.6 (95% CI 1.1–2.4). All participants in the second cohort
were different from those in the first.

## Author conclusion C1

Independent clinical validation places this result at the highest causal
evidence level: X drives response and should determine which treatment a
patient receives.
