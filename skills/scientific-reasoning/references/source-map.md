# Scientific Rule Sources and Maintenance

Last source verification: 2026-09-07. Rule revision: 2026-09-08.
This ledger links maintained decisions to primary research or official sources;
it is not an exhaustive literature review, a claim of current clinical advice,
or a copy of publisher full text. Original rule summaries are used here.

| Decision supported | Primary/official source | Scope and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Treatment-effect predictive biomarker versus outcome prediction | [FDA–NIH BEST, Predictive Biomarker](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK402283/) | A one-arm association can predict outcomes in that setting but does not alone establish differential treatment benefit; specify the comparator and effect scale |
| Prognostic association is context-specific | [FDA–NIH BEST, Prognostic Biomarker](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK402289/) | Population, baseline/background treatment and endpoint matter; prognosis is not treatment-effect modification |
| Biological replication in single-cell inference | [Squair et al., 2021](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-25960-2) | Benchmark supports accounting for biological replicates; pseudobulk is a practical default, not universally optimal under every estimand |
| Clone/state, selection/induction and stable non-genetic resistance | [Shaffer et al., 2017](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature22794) | Model-specific experimental demonstration; not proof that every therapy-associated state follows this process |
| Expression-based communication is a hypothesis sensitive to method/resource | [Dimitrov et al., 2022](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-022-30755-0) | Transcript measurements and database priors do not establish protein availability, contact or functional signaling |
| Batch removal and biological conservation are separate aims | [Luecken et al., Nature Methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-021-01336-8) | Integration benchmarks are task-specific; good mixing does not repair unidentifiable study designs |

The four-axis evidence profile, falsifier table, optional expert lenses and
portable handoff are this skill's author-designed decision aids. They are not
published grading standards or validated diagnostic instruments. A controlled
perturbation, external association and clinical utility inform different axes;
do not transform them into a single E0–E5 hierarchy.

Before a material scientific release, recheck affected official definitions,
primary papers and corrections; record which rule changed and why. Prefer a
new discriminating counterexample or experiment over accumulating citations.
For a new factual clinical/biological claim, verify a current primary source at
use time. Leave unavailable verification explicit without blocking other
reasoning. Purely documentary evaluation cases are not empirical validation.
